How it works

Takeover SMTX connects people who need to leave their San Marcos lease with people looking for a place. It's free for both.

Leaving your lease

  1. Check with your complexAsk the leasing office whether your lease can be transferred and what the fee is.
  2. List it freeTakes about 2 minutes. Add photos and the transfer fee so people know what to expect. Most listings go live within minutes, and we email you your link.
  3. We verify itAdd a screenshot of your lease when you list (it stays private and gets deleted after we check it). Verified listings get more trust and more messages.
  4. Share your linkPost your listing's link on your story and in group chats. Shared listings get taken faster.
  5. Get matchedWhen someone taps "I'm interested," we email you their name and number, and they get your contact info. We also send your listing to people on our alert list it fits.
  6. Hand it offYou both go through your complex's transfer process. We check in every two weeks, and one tap takes your listing down once it's taken.

Looking for a place

  1. Browse takeoversFilter by price, move-in date, shared or whole unit, and listings with cash incentives.
  2. Tap "I'm interested"Share your name and number to see the lister's contact info. They get your info too, so either of you can reach out. Ask questions and set up a tour.
  3. Apply with the complexThe complex approves the transfer and handles the paperwork, same as any lease.
  4. Move inOften for less than a new lease, sometimes with cash from the person leaving.
  5. Nothing fits yet?Join the alert list and we'll text or email you when a matching takeover is listed, based on your budget, move-in date and roommate situation.

Takeover vs. sublease

Every listing says which one it is. Here's the difference:

TakeoverSublease
Whose name is on the leaseYours. The lease transfers to you.Still the original tenant's.
Who you pay rent toThe complexUsually the original tenant
Original tenant's responsibilityReleased (once the complex approves)Still responsible if rent isn't paid
Complex approvalRequired either way. Most complexes charge a transfer or sublease fee.

Stay safe

  • Tour the place in person, or by live video call, before committing.
  • For takeovers, pay rent and fees to the complex, not to a stranger.
  • Never send a deposit to hold a place before the complex has approved you.
  • If something feels off, hit Report on the listing and we'll look into it.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Listing a lease and browsing are both free.

Do you guarantee my lease gets taken?

No. We get your listing in front of people who are looking, but we can't promise someone takes it. Pricing it at or below what the complex charges for new leases, adding photos, and offering a small incentive all help a lot.

Do you handle the paperwork?

No. Your complex handles the transfer and approves the new tenant. We don't negotiate leases or act as an agent for anyone.

Are you part of Texas State or an apartment complex?

No. Takeover SMTX is an independent board run by a Texas State student. It isn't affiliated with the university or any property.

Who sees my phone number?

Listers: your contact info isn't posted on the site. It's only shown to people who tap "I'm interested" and share their own name and number first. People looking: your name and number go only to the lister you're interested in.

How do I edit my listing?

Use the "Edit your listing" link in any email we sent you. Lost it? Tap "Already listed? Edit or remove your listing" at the bottom of the homepage, enter your email, and we'll send it again.

How do I take my listing down?

We email you every two weeks to check if it's still available. Tap "It's been taken" and it comes down right away. You can also reply to any of our emails, or use the Feedback button, and we'll remove it.
