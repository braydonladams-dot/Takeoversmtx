How it works

Lease takeovers, made simple.

Takeover SMTX connects people who need to leave their San Marcos lease with people looking for a place. Browsing is free, and listing is free for 7 days, then $7.99/month.

  1. 1
    Check with your complex

    Ask the leasing office if your lease can be transferred and what the fee is.

  2. 2
    List it

    Sign in with Google or your email, add the details and photos, and start your 7-day free trial. After that it's $7.99/month until it's taken, and you can cancel anytime.

  3. 3
    Get it seen

    We send it to people on our alert list it fits and post it on @takeoversmtx. Want more eyes on it? Get it featured for $10.

  4. 4
    Hear from people

    When someone taps "I'm interested," you get their name and number, and it shows up in your account.

  5. 5
    Hand it off

    You both go through your complex's transfer process. Then tap "Mark taken" in your account and your listing comes down.

List your lease
  1. 1
    Browse takeovers

    Filter by price, move-in date, room type and roommates. Green tags mean the person leaving is offering cash.

  2. 2
    Tap "I'm interested"

    You get the lister's contact, and they get your name and number, so either of you can reach out. Ask questions and set up a tour.

  3. 3
    Apply with the complex

    The complex approves you and handles the paperwork, same as any lease.

  4. 4
    Move in

    Often for less than a new lease, and sometimes with cash from the person leaving.

  5. 🔔
    Nothing fits yet?

    Turn on free alerts and we'll text or email you the moment a place fits your budget and move-in date.

Takeover or sublease?

Every listing says which one it is. Either way, the complex has to approve it.

Takeover
  • The lease moves into your name
  • You pay rent to the complex
  • The person leaving is released once the complex approves
Sublease
  • The lease stays in their name
  • You usually pay rent to them
  • They're still responsible if rent isn't paid

Stay safe

👀

Tour the place in person, or on a live video call, before you commit.

🏢

Pay rent and fees to the complex, not to a stranger.

🚫

Never send a deposit to "hold" a place before the complex approves you.

⚑

Something feels off? Tap Report on the listing and we'll look into it.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Browsing, alerts and "I'm interested" are free. Listing your lease is free for 7 days, then $7.99/month plus tax until you cancel. Marking your listing taken cancels it automatically. The $10 Instagram feature is an optional one-time extra. If you listed before the monthly fee started, your listing stays free.

How do I cancel?

Open your account (your initial in the top corner) and tap Billing, or just mark your listing taken. Either way you won't be charged again.

Do I need an account?

Only to list your lease. It's one tap with Google, or a code sent to your email. Browsing, alerts and "I'm interested" work without one.

What's the $10 Instagram feature?

An optional one-time payment that gives your listing its own slide in that week's Featured places post on @takeoversmtx. See what you get.

Do you guarantee my lease gets taken?

No. We get your listing in front of people who are looking, but no one can promise someone takes it. Good photos, a fair price and a small incentive all help a lot.

Do you handle the paperwork?

No. Your complex handles the transfer and approves the new tenant. We don't negotiate leases or act as an agent for anyone.

Who sees my phone number?

It's never posted on the site. Listers' contact info only goes to people who tap "I'm interested" and share their own name and number first. People looking only share theirs with the lister they're interested in.

How do I edit my listing or mark it taken?

Sign in and open your account (your initial in the top corner). Every listing has Edit and Mark taken buttons. The links in our emails work too.

Are you part of Texas State or an apartment complex?

No. Takeover SMTX is an independent board run by a Texas State student. It isn't affiliated with the university or any property.
