Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Takeover Leases (takeoverleases.com) is a listing board that connects people who need to leave their lease with people looking for a place. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get it deleted. The short version: we only collect what we need to make listings and connections work, we never sell your information, and your contact info is only shared with people you choose to connect with.

What we collect

  • When you list a place: your name, email, how people should reach you (phone number or social handle), the listing details (complex, rent, dates, room info, description), photos you upload, and, if you give it, your Instagram handle.
  • When you tap "I'm interested": your name, phone number, and email if you add it.
  • When you sign up for alerts: your email or phone number, your budget, move-in timing, roommate preference, and any notes you add.
  • When you create an account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We don't receive your Google password or anything else in your Google account.
  • When you pay (your listing plan or a feature): payments are handled by Stripe. We see that a payment happened, which listing it's for, and your card's brand and last 4 digits so you can recognize it in your account, but never your full card number.
  • Feedback and reports you send us.
  • Visit statistics: we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count page visits and button taps. It doesn't use cookies or build a profile of you.

How we use it

  • To show your listing on the board, with your first name only.
  • To connect people: when someone taps "I'm interested," we share their name and number with the lister, and the lister's contact with them.
  • To send you alerts, listing updates, check-ins and sign-in codes.
  • To check listings for scams and spam.
  • To post listings on our Instagram (@takeoverleases). Posts show the listing details and photos, and tag you only if you gave us your handle.
  • To run your account, so you can see your listings, who's interested, and your alerts.

Who else sees it

  • The public: listing details, photos and your first name. Your contact info is never shown publicly.
  • People you connect with: through "I'm interested," as described above.
  • Services that help us run the site: Google (where listings and emails are stored and sent), Supabase (accounts), Brevo (some emails), Stripe (payments) and GoatCounter (visit counts). They only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • We never sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.
  • We may share information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Listings come down when they're taken or the lease ends. To delete your listing, alerts, account, or anything else we have about you, email team@takeoversmtx.com and we'll take care of it. You can also take a listing down yourself with the link in your listing email.

Other things to know

  • Takeover Leases is meant for college students and other adults. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
  • We use reasonable safeguards, but no website can promise perfect security.
  • If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.

Questions? Email team@takeoversmtx.com.
