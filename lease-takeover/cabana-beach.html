Questions

How do I get out of my Cabana Beach lease?

Ask the Cabana Beach leasing office whether your lease can be transferred to someone else and what the transfer fee is. Then list your lease on Takeover Leases (the first 7 days are free). Students looking for a place get alerted when it fits their budget and contact you directly. The new person applies with Cabana Beach like any other transfer.

Can I take over a lease at Cabana Beach?

Yes, when someone is leaving. Every takeover listed above is from a current resident. Tap one to see the photos, the details and how to contact them. You still apply and get approved through the Cabana Beach office.

What if nothing is listed at Cabana Beach right now?

Turn on free alerts with your budget and move-in date. We text or email you the moment a takeover that fits is listed at Cabana Beach or anywhere else in San Marcos.

Does it cost anything?

No. Listing your lease and browsing takeovers on Takeover Leases are both free.
