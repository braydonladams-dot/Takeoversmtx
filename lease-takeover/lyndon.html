Questions

How do I get out of my The Lyndon lease?

Ask the The Lyndon leasing office whether your lease can be transferred to someone else and what the transfer fee is. Then list your lease free on Takeover SMTX. Students looking for a place get alerted when it fits their budget and contact you directly. The new person applies with The Lyndon like any other transfer.

Can I take over a lease at The Lyndon?

Yes, when someone is leaving. Every takeover listed above is from a current resident. Tap one to see the photos, the details and how to contact them. You still apply and get approved through the The Lyndon office.

What if nothing is listed at The Lyndon right now?

Turn on free alerts with your budget and move-in date. We text or email you the moment a takeover that fits is listed at The Lyndon or anywhere else in San Marcos.

Does it cost anything?

No. Listing your lease and browsing takeovers on Takeover SMTX are both free.
