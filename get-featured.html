Questions

Is the feature part of the monthly fee?

No. Listing is free for 7 days, then $7.99/month. The Instagram feature is a separate, optional one-time $10 for people who want more eyes on their place.

Does a feature guarantee my lease gets taken?

No one can promise that, but more people seeing your place means more chances someone who fits will reach out.

What if my place gets taken before the post goes up?

Let us know and we'll take it out of the post.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's a one-time $10 for a spot in that week's Featured places post. Want to be in another week's post too? Just message us.
