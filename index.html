Takeover vs. sublease

In a takeover, the lease moves into your name and the original tenant is off the hook. In a sublease, they stay on the lease and you pay them. The complex must approve either way, and each listing shows its transfer fee.

How it works

Takeover SMTX is an independent listing board. It is not affiliated with Texas State University or any apartment complex. We don't negotiate leases, and every takeover must be approved by the property.
